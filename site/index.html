About

I like robots and calm, debuggable software.

I’m interested in autonomy problems where software has to make sense of a noisy world and still choose a safe action.

I also care about the less glamorous parts: useful logs, clear boundaries, sensible fallbacks, and documentation that helps the next person.

I’m currently studying electrical and computer engineering at Clarkson University. Before that, I studied artificial intelligence and machine learning in Bengaluru.
