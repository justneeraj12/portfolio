## Figures and source check

1. **Offer-letter receipt:** exact arithmetic from the illustrative ₹6 lakh CTC example; it does not represent a real person or company.
2. **Two denominators:** a hypothetical single seat beside Infosys's FY2026 company-wide employee-cost ratio. The visual explicitly says they measure different things.
3. **₹8 lakh client-price test:** two separate starting cash salaries translated into direct cost per billed hour under the essay's hypothetical billing scenario. It is arithmetic, not a market price observation or a forecast of client response.
4. **Where a better paycheck can go:** a conditional flow map for a 100,000-worker illustration. It does not assign a spending percentage or claim a GDP multiplier.
5. **Where the raise travels:** an interactive world-map allocation of a hypothetical client bill, with wage, pass-through and rent controls. It uses the RBI destination mix as a modeling assumption, not actual Bengaluru contract shares or country GDP forecasts.
6. **Where did the hour go?:** an evidence path for a disputed late-night hour, not a claim that any one digital trace automatically proves overtime.
